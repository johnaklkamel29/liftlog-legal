Heavy Day

A workout tracker for iPhone: log your sessions, follow a plan, track your progress, and train with friends or a personal trainer.

Get help

Email john.akl.kamel29@gmail.com with questions, problems or feedback. We usually reply within 24 hours.

Report a person or post

In the app, choose Report from their profile, or from the post in the activity feed. We review every report within 24 hours.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and data. If you can’t sign in, email us from the address on your account and we’ll delete it for you.
