Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

This policy explains what Heavy Day collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. Heavy Day is run by the developer named on its App Store listing. Contact us at any time at john.akl.kamel29@gmail.com.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

In short: Heavy Day has no ads and no third-party analytics, and it doesn’t track you across apps or websites. We don’t sell your data or use it for advertising or marketing. We only store health information, such as your body weight, if you agree to it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a unique account ID. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple gives us an email address (which can be a private relay address) and, the first time, your name. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored only in hashed form. We never see them. Guest accounts have no email or name, just a random ID.
  • Profile: the name, username, bio and profile photo you choose, your training goal, experience, weekly target and training style, and your privacy settings.
  • Training data: your workouts, plans, sessions (exercises, sets, weights, reps, times, effort ratings and notes), personal records, custom exercises and app settings.
  • Social activity: friend requests and friends, reactions, comments, the activity feed items created from your sessions and records, the phase recaps you choose to post, notifications, the people you block, and reports you make or that are made about your content. A report includes a copy of the reported content.
  • Coaching: if you use trainer tools, your business name, trainer code, the clients connected to you and the private notes you write about them. If you connect with a trainer, that connection and the access level you chose.
  • Health information, only with your consent: your body weight (optional, at sign-up) and the weekly check-ins you send a trainer (body weight, how you slept, how training felt and a note). A trainer’s private notes about a client can also contain health information, such as an injury.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider processes your IP address and request logs to run and protect the service, and keeps them for a short time. We don’t use them to track you or to find your location.

Your profile photo is the only photo we receive. You choose it with Apple’s photo picker, and Heavy Day can’t see the rest of your library. Rest-timer alerts, and the rest timer on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, are run by your phone. We don’t collect push notification tokens.

To work offline, Heavy Day keeps a copy of your data on your phone. Your sign-in session is kept in the iOS Keychain.

How we use it

  • To provide Heavy Day: saving and syncing your training, calculating progress and personal records, and the social features you choose to use.
  • To keep Heavy Day safe: preventing abuse, filtering objectionable content, reviewing reports and enforcing our Terms of Use.
  • To reply when you contact us.

We don’t use your information for direct marketing, and we don’t send marketing emails. The only emails we send are about your account, such as confirming your email address or resetting your password.

Who can see your information

You decide in the app, under You → Privacy. Your profile, activity, personal records and friends list can each be public, visible to friends, or visible only to you. Each session can also be hidden. People who can see your sessions can also compare their training with yours, using totals from the sessions you share. When people search for you, they can see your name, username and photo. People you block can’t see anything of yours. Guest accounts are never visible to other people.

If you connect with a personal trainer, they can see your profile, every session (including ones hidden from friends), personal records, workouts, plans, progress and the weekly check-ins you send (including your body weight) for as long as you stay connected. If you give them edit access, they can also create and change your workouts and plans. You can change their access or remove them at any time under You → Trainer, and they lose access straight away. Trainers are independent of Heavy Day and responsible for how they use your information.

Health information

Body weight and check-ins are health information, which the law treats as sensitive. We only collect them after you agree in the app, and our database won’t accept them without your consent. We use them only to show your progress and, for check-ins, to share them with a trainer you choose to connect with.

You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings → Privacy → Health information. That deletes your body weight and all your check-ins straight away.

If you use trainer tools, only record health information about a client that they have agreed to share with you, and only use it to coach them.

None of your information, including your training data, is used for advertising or marketing, sold, or shared with data brokers. Heavy Day does not read from or write to Apple Health.

Using Heavy Day without your name

You can use Heavy Day as a guest without giving us your name or email address, and your display name doesn’t have to be your real name. Some features, such as friends and personal trainers, need an account.

Service providers and overseas storage

Supabase, Inc. hosts our database, sign-in and photo storage, in Tokyo, Japan. Resend, Inc. sends our emails, such as sign-up confirmations and password resets, from servers in Tokyo, Japan, so it receives your email address and the content of those emails. Supabase and Resend are US companies, and their staff may access information from the United States to run and support their services. If we turn on CAPTCHA to stop automated sign-ups, Cloudflare, Inc. checks your device and connection from its nearest server. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple handles that sign-in.

This means your personal information is disclosed overseas, mainly to Japan and the United States. These providers are bound by contracts to keep it secure and to use it only on our behalf and under our instructions, and we take reasonable steps to make sure they handle it in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. We don’t share your personal information with anyone else unless the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, photo, training data, friends, reactions, comments and feed activity from our database straight away. Copies in our hosting provider’s backups are removed when those backups expire, within 30 days. If you used Sign in with Apple, we also revoke Heavy Day’s access to your Apple ID.

We keep reports about other people’s content, without linking them to your deleted account, where we need them for safety records. If you sign out of a guest account, its data is deleted. Withdrawing your health consent deletes your body weight and check-ins straight away.

Seeing and correcting your information

You can see and correct most of your information in the app at any time, and delete it all by deleting your account. To ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, or to correct something you can’t change yourself, email john.akl.kamel29@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days and won’t charge you.

Complaints

If you have a complaint about how we have handled your personal information, email john.akl.kamel29@gmail.com with the details. We will acknowledge it within 7 days and aim to resolve it within 30 days.

If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

Children

Heavy Day is only for people aged 16 and over, and everyone confirms their age when they sign up. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If we learn that someone under 16 has an account, we will delete it and its data. If you believe this has happened, contact us.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Our database uses row-level security, so each account can only reach the data it is allowed to see. Your sign-in session is stored in the iOS Keychain. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires.

Changes to this policy

We will update the date at the top of this policy when it changes. If a change is significant, we will ask you to review it in the app.
