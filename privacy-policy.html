Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

This policy explains what Heavy Day collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. Heavy Day is run by the developer named on its App Store listing. Contact us at any time at john.akl.kamel29@gmail.com.

In short: Heavy Day has no ads and no third-party analytics, and it doesn’t track you across apps or websites. We don’t sell your data or use your training data for advertising or marketing.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a unique account ID. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple gives us an email address (which can be a private relay address) and, the first time, your name. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored only in hashed form. We never see them. Guest accounts have no email or name, just a random ID.
  • Profile: the name, username, bio and profile photo you choose, your training goal, experience, weekly target and training style, and your privacy settings.
  • Training data: your workouts, plans, sessions (exercises, sets, weights, reps, times, effort ratings and notes), personal records, custom exercises and app settings.
  • Social activity: friend requests and friends, reactions, the activity feed items created from your sessions and records, the phase recaps you choose to post, notifications, the people you block, and reports you make or that are made about your content. A report includes a copy of the reported content.
  • Coaching: if you use trainer tools, your business name, trainer code, the clients connected to you and the private notes you write about them. If you connect with a trainer, that connection, the access level you chose, and the weekly check-ins you send (body weight, how you slept, how training felt and a note).
  • Technical data: our hosting provider processes your IP address and request logs to run and protect the service, and keeps them for a short time. We don’t use them to track you or to find your location.

Your profile photo is the only photo we receive. You choose it with Apple’s photo picker, and Heavy Day can’t see the rest of your library. Rest-timer alerts are scheduled on your phone. We don’t collect push notification tokens.

To work offline, Heavy Day keeps a copy of your data on your phone. Your sign-in session is kept in the iOS Keychain.

How we use it

  • To provide Heavy Day: saving and syncing your training, calculating progress and personal records, and the social features you choose to use.
  • To keep Heavy Day safe: preventing abuse, filtering objectionable content, reviewing reports and enforcing our Terms of Use.
  • To reply when you contact us.

If you’re in the UK or European Economic Area, we rely on these legal bases: performing our contract with you (running the app), our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and safe, and complying with the law.

Who can see your information

You decide in the app, under You → Privacy. Your profile, activity, personal records and friends list can each be public, visible to friends, or visible only to you. Each session can also be hidden. When people search for you, they can see your name, username and photo. People you block can’t see anything of yours. Guest accounts are never visible to other people.

If you connect with a personal trainer, they can see your profile, every session (including ones hidden from friends), personal records, workouts, plans, progress and the weekly check-ins you send (including your body weight) for as long as you stay connected. If you give them edit access, they can also create and change your workouts and plans. You can change their access or remove them at any time under You → Trainer, and they lose access straight away. Trainers are independent of Heavy Day and responsible for how they use your information.

Service providers

We use Supabase, Inc. to host our database, sign-in and file storage. Supabase processes your data only on our behalf and under our instructions. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple handles that sign-in. We don’t share your personal data with anyone else unless the law requires it, or to protect people’s safety.

Health and fitness data

Your training data is used only to provide Heavy Day. It is never used for advertising or marketing, sold, or shared with data brokers. Heavy Day does not read from or write to Apple Health.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, photo, training data, friends, reactions and feed activity from our database straight away. Copies in our hosting provider’s backups are removed when those backups expire, within 30 days. If you used Sign in with Apple, we also revoke Heavy Day’s access to your Apple ID.

We keep reports about other people’s content, without linking them to your deleted account, where we need them for safety records. If you sign out of a guest account, its data is deleted.

Your rights

You can view and correct your information in the app at any time, and delete it by deleting your account. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to request a copy of your data, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To make a request, email john.akl.kamel29@gmail.com. We will respond within one month.

We don’t sell or “share” personal information as those terms are defined in California law.

Children

Heavy Day isn’t for children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect data from children under 13, or under the minimum age where you live. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Our database uses row-level security, so each account can only reach the data it is allowed to see. Your sign-in session is stored in the iOS Keychain. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

International transfers

Your data is stored on Supabase servers, which may be in a different country from yours. Where the law requires it, transfers are protected by safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

Changes to this policy

We will update the date at the top of this policy when it changes. If a change is significant, we will ask you to review it in the app.

Contact
